This Privacy Policy explains how Pacemaker Career Consulting ("Pacemaker", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use our website, online courses, ebooks, and workshops (the "Services"). We handle personal information in line with Canada's _Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act_ (PIPEDA) and other privacy laws that apply to us.

## 1. Who Is Responsible

Pacemaker Career Consulting is responsible for the personal information it collects. Our Privacy Officer oversees our privacy practices and handles privacy questions, requests, and complaints. You can contact the Privacy Officer at [Hello@pacemakerca.com](mailto:Hello@pacemakerca.com) (subject: "Privacy").

## 2. Information We Collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account information:** your first and last name, email address, password (stored by our sign-in provider, not by us), nickname, and profile image.
- **Preferences:** the career interests you select.
- **Your content:** reviews you write and messages you send us.

**Information created when you use the Services**

- **Learning activity:** the course videos you watch and your viewing progress, your favourites, and the items in your cart.
- **Purchases:** what you bought, the amount paid, any promo code used, payment status, and refunds. Your card details are collected by Stripe; we receive only a payment reference and receipt.
- **Workshops:** the workshops you register for.
- **Sign-in and technical information:** when you last signed in, and technical data such as your IP address, browser, and device that our service providers collect to keep sign-in, payments, and video playback secure and working.

**Information from others**

- If you sign up with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile image with us.
- Stripe tells us whether a payment succeeded, failed, or was refunded.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

- create and manage your account and sign you in securely;
- process payments, give you access to what you bought, and manage workshop seats and refunds;
- show your learning progress, favourites, and cart;
- recommend courses, ebooks, and workshops that match your interests;
- display your reviews;
- respond to your questions and send service messages, such as receipts and workshop updates;
- prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and
- meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Consent

We collect, use, and disclose personal information with your consent, except where the law allows otherwise. By creating an account and using the Services, you consent to the uses described in this policy that are needed to provide the Services.

We will ask for your separate, express consent before sending you newsletters or promotional emails. Every promotional email will include a way to unsubscribe, and we will act on your request within 10 business days.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal or contractual limits. If you withdraw consent that we need to provide a Service, we may no longer be able to provide it, and we will explain what that means for you.

## 5. Who We Share Information With

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the Services, and only as much as they need:

- **Clerk**: sign-in and account management.
- **Google**: sign-in, if you choose to use your Google account.
- **Supabase**: database hosting for account, learning, and order information.
- **Amazon Web Services (S3)**: storage of files such as images and ebooks.
- **Stripe**: payment processing, receipts, refunds, and fraud prevention.
- **Wistia**: course video hosting and playback.

These providers may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it. We may also disclose personal information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of Pacemaker, our users, or others, or as part of a merger, sale, or transfer of our business, in which case the recipient must continue to protect it under this policy.

## 6. Storage and Processing Outside Canada

Some of our service providers store or process personal information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is outside Canada, your information is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its courts, law enforcement, and national security authorities. We use contracts and other measures to require these providers to protect it.

## 7. How Long We Keep Information

- **Account and learning information:** while your account is open. After you ask us to close your account, we delete or anonymize it within 30 days, unless we need to keep it to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation.
- **Purchase and payment records:** for at least six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for Canadian tax and accounting purposes.
- **Reviews:** until you delete them or close your account.
- **Messages you send us:** for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.

## 8. How We Protect Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards suited to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls that limit personal information to people who need it, and reputable service providers with their own security programs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by law.

## 9. Your Rights

You can ask to:

- see the personal information we hold about you and learn how we have used and shared it;
- correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- withdraw your consent; and
- close your account and have your personal information deleted, subject to the retention periods above.

Send requests to [Hello@pacemakerca.com](mailto:Hello@pacemakerca.com). We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond within 30 days, or tell you if we need more time and why. You can update some information yourself on My Page.

## 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are needed to run the Services:

- **Sign-in:** Clerk uses cookies to keep you signed in and protect your account.
- **Payments:** Stripe uses cookies on its checkout pages to process payments and prevent fraud.
- **Video playback:** Wistia uses cookies and similar technologies to play videos and measure playback, such as how much of a video has been watched.

We do not currently use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Services, such as sign-in, may then stop working.

## 11. Children

The Services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 16 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Questions and Complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we handle your personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can file a complaint with the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca).

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If we make a material change, we will notify you by email or on the Services before it takes effect, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
